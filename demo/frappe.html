gantt-a11y demo with frappe-gantt

The same engine as the dhtmlx demo, with an adapter for frappe-gantt. frappe-gantt has no keyboard navigation of its own, so the adapter adds the task list on the left: Tab into it, move with Up and Down.

Keys as in the dhtmlx demo (Shift+H speaks them). Not available here: the task form (frappe-gantt has none), link types other than finish to start, and tasks inside tasks.

In the data: "Launch" depends on four tasks, "Budget approval" is read-only.
