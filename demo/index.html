gantt-a11y demo

Press Tab to enter the chart and move between tasks with the arrow keys. Shift+H speaks all keys. Step-by-step checks: TESTING.md.

Reading: Shift+D dependencies, Shift+O same time, Shift+B ends before, Shift+A starts after (press again for more). Moving focus: Shift+P / Shift+S. Changing: Shift+J earlier, Shift+K later, Shift+I longer, Shift+M shorter. Links: Shift+L on one task, then on another; Shift+T type; Shift+U twice after a jump removes it. Shift+R repeats. Enter opens the task form, Ctrl+Enter adds a subtask.

In the data: "Launch" depends on five tasks, "Go-live" is a milestone, "Budget approval" is read-only, "Testing" is 40 percent done.
