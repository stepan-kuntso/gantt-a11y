Demo: dhtmlx-gantt

dhtmlx-gantt 10.0.3 Community Edition with its keyboard navigation, with and without gantt-a11y.

The chart

Press Tab to move into the chart, then the arrow keys. In the data, "Launch" depends on five tasks, "Go-live" is a milestone, "Budget approval" is read-only. Reload the page to restore the data.

on: the chart speaks the extension's answers

Shift+J, K, I and M form a cross on a QWERTY keyboard: earlier, later, longer, shorter. The library's own keys still work: Enter opens the task form, Control+Enter adds a subtask.
