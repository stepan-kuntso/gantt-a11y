Demo: frappe-gantt

frappe-gantt 1.2.2 has no keyboard support of its own. With gantt-a11y, a task list next to the chart takes the focus and the same commands work on it.

The chart

Press Tab to move into the task list, then up and down. Not available here: the task form (frappe-gantt has none), link types other than finish to start, and tasks inside tasks. Reload the page to restore the data.

on: the chart speaks the extension's answers

Keys

Keys on a focused task in the list
KeysCommand
Reading
Shift+DDependencies
Shift+OSame time
Shift+BEnds before
Shift+AStarts after
Orientation
Shift+WWhere am I
Shift+VOverview of the chart
Moving focus
Shift+PTo what this depends on
Shift+STo what depends on this
Shift+CNext conflict
Shift+ZBack to where the jumps started
Changing
Shift+JEarlier
Shift+KLater
Shift+ILonger
Shift+MShorter
Links
Shift+LMake a link
Shift+TLink type
Shift+URemove link
Other
Shift+RRepeat last
Shift+HHelp

Shift+J, K, I and M form a cross on a QWERTY keyboard: earlier, later, longer, shorter. Without the extension the list is not there, and the chart cannot be reached from the keyboard.
