How to try with a screen reader

Open one of the demos, move into the chart, and ask it questions.

What has been checked, all by the author, a sighted developer: the VoiceOver steps with Safari 26.2 on macOS 26.2, in a full pass of 103 steps; the NVDA steps with NVDA 2026.3.1 and Chrome 157 on Windows, in a partial check. The JAWS steps follow how JAWS usually handles web applications; they have not been checked with this extension, and neither has Firefox. No one who uses a screen reader every day has tried it yet. If something does not work as described, that is useful to know.

Everywhere

  1. Open the dhtmlx-gantt demo. Make sure the switch says the extension is on.
  2. Move to the chart with Tab, not with a mouse click. Focus lands on the first task row.
  3. Move between tasks with the up and down arrow keys. Each row says the task's name, dates, duration and progress.
  4. Press Shift+H for the list of keys, and again within five seconds for the next group.
  5. Good first questions: on "Launch", Shift+D (what it depends on); on "Testing", Shift+O (what runs at the same time); anywhere, Shift+V (overview).

VoiceOver on a Mac

  1. Use Safari. Turn VoiceOver on with Command+F5.
  2. If arrow keys move the VoiceOver cursor instead of the chart's focus, turn Quick Nav off with Left Arrow+Right Arrow pressed together.
  3. In Safari, Tab may skip buttons; Option+Tab reaches every control.
  4. Results of jumps and of the task form are heard first, as part of the row; answers to questions and results of keyboard edits come after the row is read.

NVDA on Windows (partial check, NVDA 2026.3.1, Chrome 157)

  1. Use Chrome (checked with Chrome 157); Firefox not checked.
  2. Move to the chart with Tab. In the check, NVDA switched to focus mode on its own when focus entered the chart.
  3. In focus mode the Shift+letter keys reach the extension. Try Shift+H: it reads the list of keys.
  4. If the keys do nothing, or NVDA moves through the page instead (browse-mode quick keys), NVDA is in browse mode. Press NVDA+Space to switch to focus mode, then try again.
  5. Known differences from VoiceOver: after Shift+J, K, I or M the new dates are heard twice (first the re-drawn row, then the result); about 5 seconds after a jump or a form result, the row is read again; NVDA says "not selected" and a row position that can change, both from the chart library's own markup.

JAWS on Windows (not yet tested)

  1. Use Chrome or Edge. Move to the chart with Tab; JAWS should turn on forms mode. If it does not, press Enter on the row, or turn the virtual cursor off with Insert+Z.

Step by step

The repository has a full list of checks with the exact expected texts (TESTING.md), and a helper page that walks through them one by one and collects your answers into a report (demo/manual-test.html; run npm run demo in the repository first).

Feedback

Two ways to say what worked and what did not: open an issue with the Screen reader feedback form on GitHub, or write to stepankuntso95@gmail.com. Which screen reader and browser you used, what you pressed, what you heard and what you expected help most; a short note is welcome too.
